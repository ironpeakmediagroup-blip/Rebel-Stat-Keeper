RED DEVIL STAT APPS — how to put them online (5 minutes)
=========================================================
These are copies of your Rebels stat apps (football, volleyball, boys + girls
basketball). They run on the same website, server and Pusher account as yours,
but send on their own channels, so a Red Devils game can never show on a Rebels
broadcast (or the other way around):
   reddevil-basketball-stats, reddevil-volleyball-stats, reddevil-football-stats

1. Go to github.com/ironpeakmediagroup-blip/Rebel-Stat-Keeper
2. Click  Add file  ->  Upload files
3. Drag the whole "reddevils" FOLDER (not just the files inside it) onto the page.
4. Click  Commit changes.  Cloudflare redeploys the site the same way it does
   when you update your own apps (about a minute).
5. Open  <your stat site address>/reddevils/  on a phone — you should see the
   red "Red Devils Stats" page with 4 sports.

Give the partner that /reddevils/ link. On the iPad: open the sport, then
Share -> Add to Home Screen. Rosters are typed in the Roster tab and saved on
that device. Don't add the Rebels apps to his devices.

Nothing in your own apps changes. Your apps keep using rebel-*-stats.

OBS side: the partner kit's Stat Report / Player of the Game graphics are
switched to the reddevil-* channels automatically when you build the kit with
--stat-prefix reddevil (see the build guide).
